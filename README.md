# Quantitative-ESG-Research-Project

This study examines the relationship between environmental, social and governance (ESG) disclosure scores and the risk adjusted performance of 14 stocks held by the Premier ETF Sri-Kehati over 2016-2025, and explores how varying ESG weightings informs portfolio optimization. The analysis uses a tabular dataset combining ESG disclosure data from Bloomberg with historical stock prices from YahooFinance. Random Forest Regression was used to determine whether ESG disclosure scores could predict the risk-adjusted performance of individual stocks one year ahead. Monte Carlo Simulations were used to explore different ESG weightings and identify portfolios with the highest modelled risk-adjusted performance. The results showed that higher overall ESG disclosure scores were associated with stronger risk-adjusted investment performance, while the relationships between individual environmental, social, and governance scores and performance were mixed. These findings suggest that ESG disclosure scores may help inform portfolio construction, although their usefulness varies across the three pillars. The results should be interpreted within the study’s limited sample and historical period, recognising that greater disclosure does not necessarily indicate stronger sustainability performance or guarantee future investment returns and that superior investment performance may also reflect factors such as company size, sector exposure, profitability, and market conditions.
